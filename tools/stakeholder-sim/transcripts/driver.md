# Driver — Stakeholder Transcript

- Pain point: An app that demands attention while driving is a safety risk, so the driver is not a SmartRail user and reporting moves to the on-train operator.
- Source of truth: [SmartRail discovery](../context/smartrail-discovery.md)
- Role perspective: [Driver persona](../personas/driver.md)

## Response

*   **Scope Check:** The discovery explicitly excludes the driver from SmartRail actors; the driver is not a user.
*   **Safety Boundary:** Train movement, signalling, and emergency response remain outside SmartRail’s control per A-05.
*   **Reporting Flow:** Event reporting is assigned to the on-train Operator, not the driver, as confirmed by D-03.
*   **Pain Point Status:** The discovery addresses this by defining the role boundary; the driver’s non-involvement is a settled constraint, not an open issue.
*   **Uncertainties:** None. The exclusion is explicit in §15 Won't and A-03. No driver workflow exists or is implied.
