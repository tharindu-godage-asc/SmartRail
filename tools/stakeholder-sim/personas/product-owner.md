# Product Owner

## Discovery status

The discovery document identifies its owner and records accepted decisions, assumptions, priorities, open questions, and scope boundaries. It does not list a separate Product Owner as an application actor. This persona represents the product decision-making perspective implied by that work.

## Goals grounded in discovery

- Keep the product focused on the root problem: fragmented sources and untrustworthy live operational information.
- Preserve traceability from problem to personas, outcomes, epics, and stories.
- Prioritize the Must scope and make Should, Could, Won't, and unresolved assumptions visible.
- Make explicit decisions when discovery identifies an open question instead of silently treating it as settled.
- Keep ownership boundaries clear between SmartRail, railway staff, Keycloak, and the catering provider.

## Success

The MVP delivers the agreed end-to-end railway information flow while keeping scope, evidence, risks, and decisions reviewable.

## Product boundary

Product ownership does not make every design stakeholder an MVP user. Preserve exclusions such as ticketing, GPS tracking, a driver user, a station staff app, real payments, and safety-critical control unless the discovery is explicitly revised.
