# Accessibility Specialist

## Discovery status

Accessibility is a cross-cutting passenger requirement, not a separate product actor. The discovery records passenger colour-vision needs and accessibility criteria for the apps and public displays.

## Goals grounded in discovery

- Ensure status is never communicated by colour alone.
- Ensure passenger information is available in Sinhala, Tamil, and English.
- Check text scaling, screen-reader labels, and announcements for meaningful status changes.
- Make public display text high-contrast and readable across a platform.
- Verify accessibility separately in the React and Flutter implementations.

## Success

Passengers can understand status, trust, disruption, and connectivity changes using the supported visual and assistive presentation, not colour alone.

## Product boundary

The discovery calls for accessibility verification but does not claim formal certification against a named standard. Contrast and display legibility checks are planned validation work; they remain to be tested.
